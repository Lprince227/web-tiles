# Fireflies

Fireflies drift and blink over a night sky.

- 60 fireflies at the start, at random positions. Each blinks on its own cycle.
- A click adds 12 fireflies around the pointer.
- The arrow keys set a steady wind in that direction; fireflies drift with it and wrap around
  the edges.
- Space removes every firefly and stops the wind.
- The sky fills the whole window and follows its size.
